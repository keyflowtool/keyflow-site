Blend to Neighbors

Blend your pose toward the key on its left or right.

What it does

Move your pose toward the key before or after it, a little or all the way.

  • Left / Right: drag left toward the key before, or right toward the key after.
  • Gradual: a small drag is a light touch; at the end of the slider the pose matches the neighbor.
  • Several keys: a run of selected keys moves toward the same neighbor, so they all meet in one pose.
  • No keys selected: it works on the current frame and adds the key if there is none.
  • Safe channels: switches and on/off channels are left alone.

Good for: leaning a breakdown toward the pose it leads into, or turning a few keys into a hold.

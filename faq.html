Questions

Before you start

Maya versions, install, the trial, licenses and refunds, in one place.

Does KeyFlow work on Mac or Linux?

No. KeyFlow runs on Windows, with Maya 2024, 2026 and 2027.

Which Maya versions are included?

Maya 2024, 2026 and 2027. One purchase covers all three, and the download includes the three builds.

How do I install it?

Unzip the download to a normal folder, then drag KeyFlow_DragDrop_Install.py into Maya's viewport. A KeyFlow shelf appears, with no restart. If Maya asks about loading plugins, choose Allow.

How does the trial work?

Install KeyFlow and start the 30-day trial inside Maya. When you buy, the same install becomes your full license, with nothing to reinstall. The trial is for evaluation, not for production work.

Do I need to be online?

Only once, to activate. After that KeyFlow works offline, with no login and no license server.

How many computers can I use?

Individual: two activations, for example your workstation and your laptop. Studio: one per seat.

What if I change computers?

Send the new machine code and your license file is reissued.

Can I give my license to someone else?

Yes. A license is not tied to the buyer: hand it to another animator, they send their machine code, and the license file is reissued for their computer. With an Individual license you can also give your second activation to an animator friend.

Are updates free?

Yes, always. KeyFlow tells you when a new version is out and can download it for you. You can skip any version.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Every paid license has a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Write to keyflowtool@gmail.com.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Maya without launching KeyFlow and drag KeyFlow_DragDrop_Uninstall.py into the viewport. It removes everything, your license key included, so keep a copy if you plan to reinstall.

Where do I get help?

Write to keyflowtool@gmail.com.
